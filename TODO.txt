cores/esp32 is of no concern - these get bundled when building for esp32.
libraries/ is of no concern for the same reason.


docs/, .github and .gitlab are also of no concern, as they contain no relative executables (however CI/CD could be set up in .github)

We can just get rid of tests/, at this early stage we don't need testing.
tools/ is what we care about.

variants/ call functions that we are already going to replace.

package/ contains the actual executables, which we care about.
